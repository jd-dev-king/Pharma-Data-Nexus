# Pharma Enterprise Analytics Platform

A synthetic PostgreSQL and Three.js portfolio project modeling pharmaceutical MES, LIMS, QMS, CMMS, training, environmental-monitoring, and ALCOA+ data.

## Scale

- 38 normalized tables
- 600 production batches
- 19,200 process parameter records
- 9,000 analytical results
- 8,000 audit events
- 120 equipment assets
- 3,600 environmental-monitoring records
- 2,160 training records

## Important limitation

This is educational synthetic data. It is not a validated GxP system and must not be used for product release, clinical decisions, regulatory submissions, or operational manufacturing decisions.
